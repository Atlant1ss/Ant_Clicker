# Ant_Clicker
A website game where you acquire ants by clicking on the main ant so you can buy special ants and improve your gameplay. Futurally, there will be more events and more ants to acquire!

# Main Page

It shows the few brief functionalities the game have, being:
| | |
|---|---|
| **Register** | Creates a new account accourding to the username, email and password. Those are going to be used to save your progress |
| **Login**    | If you want to continue the progress you saves, insert your username and password to log into the game with the amount of ants collected saved |
| **Logout**   | On the same idea, if you want to login with another account or just get out of the account you just used, just logout |
| **Leaderboard** | Using the accounts who saved their games, there is this leaderboard showing your progress, rank and username so you can compare to other players |
| **Account**  | If the account is not with the username you wanted you can go and delete it with the password, confirming its you who is deleting |

# Game System

By pressing the button **play**, its going to redirect you to the most exciting gameplay ever made!

PRESS BUTTTON TO HATCH ANTS → BUY SPECIAL ANTS → HATCH MORE ANTS

(I know, insane right?)

# Special Ants

The special ants changes the way you play your game and the amount of ants you recieve. Of course they are not so easy to obtain, everything has a cost.
(you can also see what hatching each ant does by passing the cursor through the button)

| ANT | COST | MODIFIER |
|---|---|---|
| **Worker** | 10 ants | Generates 1 ant per second. |
| **Queen** | 50 ants | Increases your click strength by 1. |
| **Soldier** | 100 ants | Increases your critical hit chance and critical damage multiplier. |
| **Explorer** | 200 ants | Randomly spawns a sugar lump on the screen. Click it for a temporary buff. |
| **Witch** | 500 ants | Increases the efficiency of your other ants by a percentage. | 
| **Corporate** | 1000 ants | Earns interest based on the number of ants you currently have stored. |
